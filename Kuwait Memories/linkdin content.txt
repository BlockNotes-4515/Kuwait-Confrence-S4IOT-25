🌏Representing India '🇮🇳 on an International Stage at KCST 🇰🇼, Kuwait✈



S4IoT'25 "THE SECOND SYMPOSIUM ON SMART, SUSTAINABLE AND SECURE INTERNET OF THINGS" 6th-7th May'2K25 at Doha District, KUWAIT

I’m extremely proud to share that I, Dhruv Dhayal, along with my teammate Pratham Aggarwal, had the incredible opportunity to represent India at the S4IO6’25 International Conference, held on 6th–7th May 2025 at Kuwait College of Science and Technology (KCST) in Doha District, Kuwait. We presented our research paper titled “IntelliGuard: IoT-Enabled Autonomous Spybot Intelligence for Real-Time Surveillance in Next-Generation Security Applications” (Paper ID: 28) — a prototype project that aims to revolutionize real-time security using IoT-powered autonomous intelligence. Showcasing our work on such a prestigious international platform was a dream come true, and a true testament to months of hard work, dedication, and learning.

This achievement wouldn’t have been possible without the constant guidance and incredible mentorship of Dr. Manzoor Ansari Sir. Beyond technical direction, he shared with us a life lesson that we will carry forever: “Without arrogance and ego, be simplicity and hard work.” These words resonated deeply and shaped the way we approached not just our project, but every challenge with humility, perseverance, and purpose. We are also sincerely thankful to our HOD, Dr. Ganesh Kumar Wadhwani Sir, whose unshakable support and encouragement made this journey smoother—especially by helping us secure travel funding at the right time. A big thank you to our Director, Dr. Rachita Rana Ma’am, for providing us the institutional support and platform to represent our work at a global level, and to our college Institute of Information Technology and Management (IITM), for fostering innovation, creativity, and technical excellence in students like us.

Representing our nation and institution on an international stage was not just a proud moment—it was a life-changing experience that has inspired us to keep building, keep innovating, and keep aiming higher. This is just the beginning of a much larger journey, and I’m truly excited for what’s to come.
